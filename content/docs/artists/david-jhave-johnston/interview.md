And then I ended up, kind of, fiddling around with software and computation, and decided I needed to go back to school, and I had built a large, sort of, repository of work on years, or one which John found it posted to the electronic literature community way back in two thousand and one, I think. The segue was that I was in literature that became visual. I was involved with multimedia theater, and then, in order to make visual literature. Obviously the computer and the gifs came along. I didn't know what a gif was, but there was -- You can make this thing with the moving words. Yes, somehow, just a natural segue.  I've just been riding the current. Basically the tide that's carrying us all the technological ...[garbled] washing us into some estuary of extinction, probably. [laughs] Yeah, that's right. Preservation and  extinction, it's kind of like a parallel thing.
